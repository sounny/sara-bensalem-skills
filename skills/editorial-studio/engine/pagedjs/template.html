Act 2: Territorial & Environmental Context

Bioclimatic Topography & Massing Logic

The project addresses the extreme diurnal temperature swings of the Atlas foothills. By partially submerging the lower gallery into existing shale bedrock, the design harnesses earth coupling to stabilize indoor mean radiant temperatures.

Deep structural reveals shade southwest fenestration against intense summer solar azimuths, while prevailing nighttime breezes are captured through passive Barjeel wind catchers and drawn across sunken water basins to achieve evaporative cooling.

Act 4: Tectonic Proof (1:20 Wall Section)

Constructive Rigor & Thermal Break Integrity

Eliminating 3D render traps demands verified constructive detailing. The exterior terracotta rain-screen is ventilated with continuous 40mm air gap, preventing moisture accumulation while providing calibrated acoustic attenuation.

Structural cantilever balconies incorporate Schöck Isokorb thermal break modules, ensuring unbroken thermal envelope insulation across structural slab edges.

Act 5: Lived Climax & Scenography

1:5 Custom Millwork & Bespoke Reveals

Bespoke walnut millwork features deliberate 3mm black shadow line reveals to accommodate seasonal hygroscopic expansion without binding or face deflection.

Integrated Blum Movento soft-close concealed hardware maintains clean planar facades, paired with dropped acoustic perimeter seals ensuring NC 30 acoustic performance.
