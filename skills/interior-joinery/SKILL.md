# Interior Joinery (`/interior-joinery`)
### *1:5 Tectonic Millwork, Shadow Reveals & Hardware Integration Engine*
**Sara Bensalem Studio • Strasbourg Atelier [48°35'05"N 07°45'02"E]**

> *"Luxury interior architecture is defined by what you do not see: the 3mm shadow reveal between stone and veneer, the precision routing of concealed hinge pockets, and the tactile transition between cool limestone and brushed French oak."*

`interior-joinery` equips coding agents with fabrication-level millwork detailing:
- **Shadow Reveals (*Joint Creux*)**: Calibrated 3mm to 10mm negative reveals eliminating sloppy silicone caulk lines at floor baseboards, ceilings, and casework.
- **Concealed Hardware Tolerances**: Blum Clip Top Blumotion / Hettich Sensys cup hinge depth ($12.8\text{ mm}$ routing, $2\text{ mm}$ door reveal gaps).
- **Substrate & Cladding Detailing**: 19mm moisture-resistant MDF core veneered with 0.8mm quartersawn French oak, anchored to concealed aluminum cleat subframing.
- **Tactile Material Triptychs**: Architectural material schedules pairing primary stone, primary timber, and patinated bronze hardware.

## 💬 How to Use in Antigravity, Claude & Cursor

```bash
# Generate 1:5 Cabinetry & Shadow Reveal Drawing:
/interior-joinery generate --scale 1:5 --joint shadow-reveal --output joinery_1_5.svg
```
