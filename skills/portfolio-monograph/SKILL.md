5. **Typeset & Format Spreads**: Apply Swiss editorial grid discipline, calibrated margins, muted chapter numbering (20% opacity), and restrained typography.

## ⚠️ The 30-Second Screening Reality & The 4 Fatal Traps

Hiring directors at top international firms (*Foster + Partners*, *BIG*, *Gensler*, *Snøhetta*, *Dar Al-Handasah*, luxury Parisian interior ateliers) review between 200 and 600 applications per opening. The initial filtering decision is made in **15 to 30 seconds**.

### The 4 Fatal Traps:
1. **The Render Trap (75% of rejections)**: Pages filled with saturated Lumion/Enscape perspectives with zero constructability proof (no wall sections, no dimension chains, no structural joints). *Diagnosis: "Can render, cannot build."*
2. **The Wall-of-Text Trap**: Long descriptive essays that no reviewer has time to read. *Remedy: Scannable metadata blocks, bulleted functional specs, and diagram callouts.*
3. **The Micro-Font / Illegible Annotation Trap**: Dimension chains exported from AutoCAD at 4pt without line-weight calibration, becoming unreadable on standard screens.
4. **The Monotonous Grid Trap**: Every page has identical 4-image grids with no rhythm, no full-bleed hero spreads, and no white space breathing room.

## 🎭 The 5-Act Spatial Case Study Framework

Every featured project in a portfolio must follow this 5-act dramaturgical structure across 2 to 4 spreads:

1. **Act I: The Hook & Project Passport** (Spread 1 Left)
   - Project Title, Location, Typology, Year, Client/Studio, Software Stack.
   - 2-sentence executive premise stating the core challenge.
2. **Act II: The Contextual Conflict & Research** (Spread 1 Right)
   - Site diagrams, climatic sun/wind paths, historical footprint, or socio-economic problem statement.
3. **Act III: The Spatial Strategy & Volumetric Evolution** (Spread 2 Left)
   - 3-to-4-step massing diagrams or exploded axonometric showing structural and circulatory logic.
4. **Act IV: The Constructive Proof (The "Anti-Trap")** (Spread 2 Right & Spread 3 Left)
   - Scaled technical floor plans ($1:50$ or $1:100$), detailed wall sections ($1:10$ or $1:20$), material callouts, and structural layers.
5. **Act V: The Atmosphere & Sensory Resolution** (Spread 3 Right / Spread 4)
   - Hero atmospheric render, material triptych, lighting study (day vs. night), and verified impact/outcome.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Execution Guide for Agents

When an agent is asked to create or audit a portfolio:

### Step 1: Run Pre-Flight Inspection
Check the candidate's PDF or project folder using the bundled script:
```bash

```

### Step 2: Determine the Optimal Archetype
Assess the user's primary discipline and career objective:
- Interior architecture & bespoke finishes $	o$ **Archetype 03 (French Luxury Minimalist)**
- Technical office, execution detailing $	o$ **Archetype 02 (Technical Office Working Set)**
- Junior architect applying to international design firms $	o$ **Archetype 01 (Swiss Editorial Landscape)**
- Social infrastructure, public master planning $	o$ **Archetype 05 (Feminist Urbanist & Social Researcher)**

### Step 3: Enforce the Swiss Grid & Typography Hierarchy
- **Header Numbers**: 48pt–72pt, 15%–20% opacity greige/neutral tint (`#D1D5DB` or `#9CA3AF`).
- **Project Titles**: 22pt–28pt, Bold or Medium, Tracking -0.02em.
- **Section Subheadings**: 11pt–13pt, All Caps or Small Caps, Tracking +0.08em.
- **Body Copy**: 8.5pt–9.5pt, Regular, Leading 13pt–15pt (never exceed 60 characters per line).
- **Drawing Annotations**: 6.5pt–7.5pt, Light or Regular, high contrast dark charcoal (`#1F2937`).

### Step 4: Validate Constructability Proof
Ensure at least **one detailed tectonic plate** exists per architectural project (e.g., exploded axonometric, construction section, or detail callout). If missing, flag as **"Critical Action Item: Missing Constructive Proof"**.

## 🏛️ Architectural Discourse & Design Languages

Sara Bensalem Skills speaks fluent, authentic architectural theory. Rather than generic corporate design tokens, it articulates space through recognized architectural movements, tectonic doctrines, and spatial concepts:

### 1. Brutalism & Béton Brut (The New Brutalism)
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Alison & Peter Smithson (*The New Brutalism*, 1955), Reyner Banham, Le Corbusier (*La Tourette*, *Chandigarh*), Louis Kahn (*Salk Institute*), Marcel Breuer.
- **Banham's Three Criteria**:
  1. *Memorability of Image*: Pure, unmistakable stereotomic silhouettes.
  2. *Clear Exhibition of Structure*: Columns, cantilever bents, and mechanical infrastructure remain legible and unclad.
  3. *Valuation of Materials "As Found"*: Raw *béton brut* with board-marked timber grain, exposed tie-rod pockets, bush-hammered granite ashlar, unpainted mass timber.
- **Portfolio Application**: Raw high-contrast Swiss typography, uncropped 1:20 constructive wall sections exposing rebar clearance and shuttering joints, zero cosmetic render fluff.

### 2. Tectonic Rationalism (The Swiss Ticino School)
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Livio Vacchini, Luigi Snozzi, Mario Botta, Aldo Rossi (*The Architecture of the City*).
- **Core Principles**:
  - The dialectic between the **stereotomic base** (heavy, earth-grounded, subtractive mass) and the **tectonic frame** (light, additive, assembled columns).
  - Mathematical proportion systems (Le Corbusier Modulor, Golden Ratio $\Phi$) translated into modular 8/12/16-column grids.
  - Strict baseline grid rhythm (8pt increments) aligning drawing captions, dimension strings, and body copy.

### 3. Phenomenological Minimalism & Material Atmosphere
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Peter Zumthor (*Atmospheres*, *Thinking Architecture*), Juhani Pallasmaa (*The Eyes of the Skin*), Alberto Campo Baeza, Sverre Fehn.
- **Core Principles**:
  - Multi-sensory tactile engagement: pairing honed limestone, quartersawn oak, and lime-hemp biotamping.
  - Spatial compression and release (*enfilade*, the compressed entrance chamber opening to a monumental light-filled hall).
  - Negative shadow reveals (*joint creux*) eliminating decorative moldings in favor of quiet, precise material boundaries.

### 4. Critical Regionalism & Ecological Vernacular
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Kenneth Frampton (*Towards a Critical Regionalism*), Geoffrey Bawa, Glenn Murcutt, Studio Mumbai.
- **Core Principles**:
  - Rejection of generic placeless glass towers in favor of tectonic site memory.
  - Hygrothermal responsiveness: local Breton granite, breathable lime mortar, deep seasonal overhangs ($65^\circ$ summer vs $18^\circ$ winter solar vectors).
  - Net-negative embodied carbon lifecycle (RE2020 compliance).

### 5. High-Tech Structural Expressionism
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Renzo Piano, Richard Rogers, Norman Foster, Peter Rice.
- **Core Principles**:
  - Clear legibility of structural load paths (tension cables, compression struts, pin connectors).
  - Articulation of Louis Kahn's *Servant vs Serviced* spaces (expressive exterior elevator cores, exposed MEP ducts).
  - Precision 1:10 cast-steel node details and triple-glazed thermal envelopes ($U_w \le 0.78\text{ W/m}^2\cdot\text{K}$).

### 6. Neoclassicism & Palladian Axial Order
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Andrea Palladio, Claude-Nicolas Ledoux, Étienne-Louis Boullée, Karl Friedrich Schinkel.
- **Core Principles**:
  - Monumental axial symmetry and the Vitruvian triad (*firmitas, utilitas, venustas*).
  - Tripartite elevation hierarchy: rusticated stereotomic ground base, heroic *piano nobile*, and pedimented attic.
  - Monumental stone porticos, Doric/Ionic column order ratios, and stereotomic colonnades.

### 7. Beaux-Arts & The Spatial Marche
- **Theoretical Lineage**: École des Beaux-Arts Paris, Charles Garnier (*Opéra Garnier*), McKim, Mead & White.
- **Core Principles**:
  - The *Parti Pris*: The unequivocal conceptual diagram that governs every subsequent spatial choice.
  - The *Marche*: The dramatic, multi-sensory promenade architecturale from entry portico, through compressed vestibule, to double-height grand stair and rotunda.
  - Expressive *poché* (solid vs void wall thickness) and monumental enfilades.

### 8. Art Nouveau & Biomorphic Iron Tectonics
- **Theoretical Lineage**: Victor Horta (*Hôtel Tassel*), Hector Guimard (*Paris Métro*), Antoni Gaudí (*Modernisme Català*).
- **Core Principles**:
  - The whiplash curve (*coup de fouet*): Structural steel and wrought iron articulated as living biomorphic stems.
  - The *Gesamtkunstwerk* (total work of art): Seamless tectonic unification of structural rib, window mullion, and bespoke door hardware.
  - Organic spatial flow, dynamic asymmetry, and luminous stained-glass light wells.

### 9. Art Deco & Streamlined Geometric Craft
- **Theoretical Lineage**: 1925 *Exposition Internationale des Arts Décoratifs* (Paris), Jacques-Émile Ruhlmann, William Van Alen (*Chrysler Building*), Raymond Hood.
- **Core Principles**:
  - Stepped ziggurat massing setbacks creating dramatic vertical towers.
  - Stylized geometric iconography: chevrons, sunburst spandrels, and streamlined reeding.
  - Luxurious material triptychs: polished Belgian black marble, satin-brushed brass, and macassar ebony casework with 1:5 shadow inlays.
