## 🏛️ Philosophy: Spatial Design Products, Not Web/Software UI

**Sara Bensalem Architectural Design Skills** is an open agentic studio and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server engineered specifically for **physical, architectural, and spatial design deliverables**.

While modern AI coding tools, Python engines, and vector libraries power generation under the hood, **these skills are not for building software UIs or web apps**. ## ⚠️ The 22 Lethal "3D Render Traps" Autopsy

Hiring partners and jury chairs reject 75% of applicants due to cosmetic 3D render traps. Our skills detect, flag, and remedy all 22 failure modes:

| # | The Lethal Render Trap | Why It Fails in Reality | How to Fix It (The Tectonic Rescue) |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **1** | **The Floating Glass Box** | Greenhouse solar overheating (>45°C), thermal shock cracking. | Recess head/sill frames into floor/ceiling; specify triple glazing with Low-E coating and motorized external venetian louvers ($\text{SHGC} \le 0.22$). |
| **2** | **The Magic Cantilever Stair** | Treads shear plaster wall on day one; code violation without handrails. | Conceal a $250 \times 100 \times 8\text{mm}$ steel box stringer inside the wall; slide solid oak sleeves over steel tubes; add structural glass guard (1.5 kN/m). |
| **3** | **The Zero-Reveal Millwork** | Seasonal hygroscopic wood expansion (2–3mm/m) causes doors to jam. | Detail a deliberate 3mm black shadow line reveal around all perimeter cabinet edges and against site plaster. |
| **4** | **The Cantilevered Stone Slab** | Natural stone has near-zero tensile strength (4–6 MPa); snaps under self-weight. | Build an internal welded RHS steel chassis; clad in lightweight aluminum honeycomb stone panels (Aerolam). |
| **5** | **The Uninsulated Rammed-Earth Wall** | Capillary groundwater suction wicks 1.2m up; frost expands 9%, causing base collapse. | Elevate earth wall 300mm on insulated hydraulic lime concrete plinth; install dual EPDM damp-proof courses and French perimeter drains. |
| **6** | **The Frameless Timber-on-Water Deck** | Capillary rot and fungal decay disintegrate timber within 18 months. | Drive hot-dip galvanized helical screw piles; elevate posts 300mm above 100-yr flood level; install continuous EPDM capillary breaks. |
| **7** | **The Sharp 90° High-Rise Tower** | Coherent cross-wind vortex shedding induces dynamic sway; shears curtain wall gaskets. | Chamfer corners with 15% building radius; add aerodynamic wind relief slots; use 4-sided structural silicone with $\pm 25\text{mm}$ drift bellows. |
| **8** | **The Unanchored Ceiling Duct** | Mechanical vibrations transmit through rigid timber walls, destroying acoustic ratings ($\text{NC} > 40$). | Provide inline silencer splitter baffles (2400mm); sleeve ducts through 50mm elastomeric acoustic collars (Sylomer); keep air speed $< 1.5\text{ m/s}$. |
| **9** | **The Full-Height Jamming Pocket Door** | Live-load ceiling slab sag (10–12mm) crushes carriage tracks; unsealed pocket leaks sound. | Install extruded aluminum deflection head channels allowing 15mm sag; add automatic drop-down acoustic seals (Athmer Schall-Ex). |
| **10** | **The Red/Green Cockpit / Dashboard** | Protanopic and deuteranopic colorblind users (8% of men) cannot see emergency alerts. | Enforce redundant visual encoding: pair color with shape geometry (triangles for caution, octagons for danger), audio chimes, and haptic pulses. |
| **11** | **The Grey-on-Grey / Beige UI** | Contrast ratio of $< 2:1$ violates WCAG 2.1 AAA, creating extreme eye strain in daylight. | Mandate minimum 7:1 contrast ratio for all analytical body text using dark titanium glassmorphic tokens (`#0B0F17` substrate, `#F1F5F9` text). |
| **12** | **The 100-Page Student Dump** | Reviewers abandon portfolio after 45 seconds due to cognitive fatigue. | Curate down to 5 flagship projects using the 5-Act Narrative; edit out school exercises; move secondary drawings to online archive links. |
| **13** | **The Placeholder Latin Leak** | Leaving `Lorem ipsum` in Table of Contents or body text signals zero quality control. | Replace with a disciplined 3-line Curatorial Statement defining candidate's specific design thesis and methodology. |
| **14** | **The Poster Screenshot Infill Hack** | Pasting vertical A1/A2 presentation boards into horizontal 16:9 slides produces 3.5pt unreadable text. | Dissect the raw vector assets; re-layout across a strict 12-column Swiss grid across 4 dedicated editorial pages. |
| **15** | **The Fake CAD Section Trap** | Labeling flat 2D unrendered wall elevations as "SECTION A-A" with zero slab or footing thickness. | Cut true building sections showing 250mm reinforced concrete slab, screed to fall, suspended ceiling plenum, and thermal insulation. |
| **16** | **The Impossible Spatial Math Error** | Gross dimensional blunders (e.g. claiming a bedroom suite is 3.1 m² when a bed alone is 3.2 m²). | Calculate exact Gross & Net Internal Areas (GIA/NIA); verify furniture clearances against PMR/ADA wheelchair turning circles. |
| **17** | **The Wasted Manufacturer Credential** | Listing Knauf, CMB, or Autodesk training on CV, but showing zero manufacturer-grade details. | Add dedicated 1:20 working detail plates citing exact manufacturer systems (Knauf W112 drywall, CMB tanking slurries). |
| **18** | **The Flattened Raster Print Trap** | Flattening vector drawings into heavy raster images destroys lineweight hierarchy and bloats file sizes. | Export vector line weights as scalable vector graphics (PDF/SVG) with calibrated line thicknesses (0.05mm hairline to 0.50mm cut). |
| **19** | **The Metadata Copy-Paste Leak** | Copy-pasting project passport metadata across disparate projects (e.g., claiming a private villa has an 18,000 m² footprint). | Bind each project passport to its verified architectural program; cross-check GIA and typology before PDF compilation. |
| **20** | **The Default Consumer PDF Metadata Trap** | Leaving consumer tool stamps ("Canva", "iLovePDF", "Smallpdf") and default export titles ("MY TECH TOGETHER", "Presentation1") in document properties. | Sanitize PDF metadata with Ghostscript or InDesign preflight; inject professional metadata (`Title: First Last - Architectural Portfolio 2026`, `Author`, `Keywords`). |
| **21** | **The 3D Book Mockup Letterbox Trap** | Embedding spreads inside a photograph or 3D render of an open physical book wastes $\ge 35\%$ of screen real estate and forces double pagination. | Export true 1:1 vector spreads (A4 landscape or 16:9) with full bleed, maximizing display canvas and lineweight resolution. |
| **22** | **Cover-to-Spread Aspect Ratio Mismatch** | Exporting a vertical cover (0.91 or 1:1.41) alongside horizontal spreads (1.40 or 16:9) causes PDF viewers to jump and rescale erratically. | Unify the entire document on a consistent aspect ratio, designing front and back covers to match the exact geometry of individual spread leaves. |
