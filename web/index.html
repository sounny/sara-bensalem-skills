            TECTONIC THEORY & DISCOURSE
          

          

            Fluent in Architectural Theory & Movements
          

          

            Sara Bensalem Skills speaks like master architects—articulating portfolios through authentic spatial concepts, stereotomic massing, tectonic truth to materials, and phenomenological light.
          

        


        
        

          
          
          
          
        


        

          
          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 01
              Reyner Banham, 1955
            

            
Brutalism & Béton Brut

            

              Tectonic honesty, unadorned stereotomic massing, board-marked shuttering seams, and valuation of materials "as found" (Alison & Peter Smithson, Le Corbusier, Louis Kahn).
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Stereotomic Volumetric Weight

              
• Truth to Materials (As-Found)

              
• Board-Formed Shuttering Ties

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 02
              Ticino School
            

            
Tectonic Rationalism

            

              Geometric discipline, strict 12/16-column Swiss modular grids, and clear articulation of stereotomic base vs tectonic frame (Livio Vacchini, Luigi Snozzi, Mario Botta).
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Stereotomic Base vs Tectonic Frame

              
• Le Corbusier Modulor & Golden Ratio

              
• Calibrated Hairline Drafting (0.13mm)

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 03
              Peter Zumthor & Pallasmaa
            

            
Phenomenological Minimalism

            

              Multi-sensory tactile engagement, spatial compression and release (*enfilade*), shadow reveals (*joint creux*), and raking chiaroscuro daylight.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Compression & Release (Thresholds)

              
• 1:5 Shadow Reveals (Joint Creux)

              
• Tactile Stone & Timber Triptychs

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 04
              Kenneth Frampton
            

            
Critical Regionalism

            

              Rejection of placeless global architecture in favor of local geological provenance, Breton granite ashlar, breathable lime-hemp biotamping, and site memory.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Geological Provenance (Local Quarries)

              
• Monolithic Breathable Bio-Composites

              
• Net-Negative Embodied Carbon (RE2020)

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 05
              Piano, Rogers & Rice
            

            
Structural Expressionism

            

              Absolute legibility of structural load transfers, exposed cast-steel nodes, tension tie-rods, and Louis Kahn's clear separation of Serviced vs Servant spaces.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Legibility of Structural Load Paths

              
• Kahnian Serviced vs Servant Hierarchy

              
• Cast Steel Nodes & Pin Connections

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 06
              Lars Müller & El Croquis
            

            
Swiss Monograph Rigor

            

              The gold standard of architectural monograph publishing. 8, 12, and 16-column modular layouts, archival bone paper (#F8F8F5), and uncropped technical plates.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• 8/12/16-Column Modular Layouts

              
• Baseline Grid Precision (8pt Ratios)

              
• Standardized Project Passports

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 07
              Palladio & Schinkel
            

            
Neoclassicism

            

              Axial symmetry, tripartite elevation hierarchy (rusticated base, piano nobile, attic), canonical Doric/Ionic orders, and Vitruvian proportional balance.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Vitruvian Triad (Firmitas, Utilitas, Venustas)

              
• Monumental Axial Porticos & Colonnades

              
• Stereotomic Rusticated Stone Base

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 08
              École des Beaux-Arts
            

            
Beaux-Arts

            

              The foundational *Parti Pris*, choreographed spatial *Marche* through monumental rotundas and grand ceremonial staircases, and expressive sectional poché.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• The Parti Pris (Founding Concept Diagram)

              
• The Marche (Choreographed Spatial Promenade)

              
• Monumental Rotundas & Ceremonial Axis

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 09
              Horta, Guimard & Gaudí
            

            
Art Nouveau

            

              Whiplash curves (*coup de fouet*), biomorphic wrought-iron structural integration, asymmetric organic elevations, and total work of art (*Gesamtkunstwerk*).
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Whiplash Curve (Coup de Fouet) Tectonics

              
• Gesamtkunstwerk (Total Work of Art)

              
• Biomorphic Wrought Iron Structural Stems

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 10
              1925 Paris Exposition
            

            
Art Deco

            

              Stepped ziggurat skyscraper setbacks, chevron and sunburst metalwork, polished black marble, and streamlined verticality paired with exotic wood veneers.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Stepped Ziggurat Massing & Setbacks

              
• Geometric Chevrons & Sunburst Reliefs

              
• Luxurious Macassar Ebony & Satin Brass Inlays

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 11
              Al-’Ula / Atelier Adeline
            

            
Ephemeral Scenography

            

              Designing for the invisible: air, memory, mist, and the dialectic between monumental desert rock and temporary petrol-blue fabric and mirrored steel.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Raw Stone vs Soft Fabric Dialectic

              
• Specular Desert Reflection & Dissolution

              
• Negative Space Restraint (Knowing When to Stop)

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 12
              Doshi, Correa & Aayojan
            

            
Indic Spatial Systems

            

              Cosmic geometry, perceptual continuum, and climate-responsive courtyard morphology rooted in ancient treatises and 100-year institutional models.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• 9-Square Vāstu-Purusha Mandala Matrix

              
• Perception Continuum (Space ↔ Mind ↔ Form)

              
• Passive Courtyard Microclimates & Jalis

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 13
              Zaha Hadid & Libeskind
            

            
Deconstructivism

            

              Fractured planes, non-rectilinear volumes, skewed structural axes, and kinetic cantilevers defying traditional Cartesian floor-wall boundaries.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Skewed Axes & Non-Euclidean Folds

              
• Moment-Frame Structural Cantilevers

              
• Dynamic Asymmetric Kinetic Tension

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 14
              Kuma, Ando & Vervoordt
            

            
Japandi & Wabi-Sabi

            

              Tactile imperfection, acoustic stillness, charred timber (*shou sugi ban*), hinoki cypress joinery, and the celebration of negative space (*Ma*).
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Shou Sugi Ban & Hinoki Wood Joinery

              
• Acoustic Washi Paper & Lime Plaster

              
• Negative Space (Ma) as Spatial Element

            

            
          


          
          

            

              MOVEMENT 15
              Haussmann, Labrouste & Liaigre
            

            
Haussmannian & Boiserie

            

              Grand *piano nobile* proportions, Point de Hongrie chevron parquet, carved marble fireplaces, and concealed flush boiserie pivot doors.
            

            

              
CONCEPTS:

              
• Point de Hongrie Chevron French Oak

              
• Concealed Boiserie Pivot Doors

              
• High-Contrast Tactile Stone Triptychs
