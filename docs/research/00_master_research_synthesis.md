## 🏛️ Key Platform Architecture Decisions Derived from Research

1. **The Trust Interface Protocol (Volume 07)**:
   - Portfolios fail when treated as art galleries or social media mood boards. The website and MCP tools must enforce the **Trust Interface standard**: proof-first project grids, outcome-forward project passports, zero-navigation tax, and radical individual attribution.

2. **The 1:20 Wall Section Mandate (Volume 02)**:
   - Evaluates portfolios specifically on the ratio of technical construction drawings to 3D renders. Candidates with >35% constructive proof achieve elite hire classification; candidates with 0 wall sections trigger the **Render Trap Alert**.

3. **Swiss Modular Column Grids (Volume 01)**:
   - Codifies 8-, 12-, and 16-column grids with strict mathematical baselines and neutral grotesk typography, eliminating cognitive fatigue during 15-second recruiter scans.

4. **The 5-Act Narrative Engine (Volume 09)**:
   - Directs AI coding assistants (Claude, Cursor, Antigravity) to sequence architectural presentations across 3–4 spreads: *The Hook & Passport*, *Context & Program*, *Spatial Anatomy*, *Tectonic Proof (1:20)*, and *Lived Climax*.

5. **Cross-Discipline Expansion Skills (Volumes 03, 04, 05, 06)**:
   - Directly informs the 3 upcoming skills in the product suite:
     - `heritage-adaptive-reuse` (Breton stone, hempcrete, truss consolidation)
     - `interior-joinery-scenography` (1:5 millwork details, shadow reveals, tactile triptychs)
     - `gender-equitable-urbanism` (Nighttime sightlines, flânerie comfort, CPTED civic safety audits)
     - `bioclimatic-diagrams` (Solar radiation, prevailing wind loops, thermal mass transfer)

6. **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Standards (Volume 10)**:
   - Establishes JSON-RPC schemas specifically structured for spatial design and physical constructibility rather than software code.
