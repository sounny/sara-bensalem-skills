## Pillar II: The 15-Second Recruiter Filter & Anti-Render-Trap Ergonomics
### Research Finding 2: The Psychology of Tier-1 Architectural Hiring Partners
Interviews with hiring partners at Foster + Partners, Herzog & de Meuron, BIG, and Renzo Piano Building Workshop reveal that **photorealistic 3D renderings (Lumion, Midjourney, Enscape) have become a net-negative trust signal**.
- **The Render Trap:** Atmospheric twilight renders obscure constructive ignorance. Recruiters spend an average of 15 to 30 seconds scanning an applicant's portfolio. If a portfolio relies solely on 3D renderings, recruiters assume the candidate cannot detail a buildable junction or navigate construction administration.
- **The Antidote (Plate IV — Constructive Proof):** A single uncropped, publication-grade 1:20 wall section with clear dimension strings, waterproofing callouts, and acoustic seals immediately establishes high constructive competence, elevating the candidate into the top 5% interview tier.

## Pillar III: Swiss Typographic Discipline & Editorial Rhythms
### Research Finding 3: The Graphic Physics of El Croquis, Detail & Lars Müller
Physical architecture portfolios fail when they adopt chaotic web layouts or dense academic text blocks. The gold standard of spatial monograph publishing is rooted in the Swiss International Style:
- **8 / 12 / 16-Column Modular Grids:**
  - **8-Column:** Optimized for narrative manifestos and full-bleed site photography.
  - **12-Column (The Le Corbusier Modulor standard):** Balanced tripartite division pairing uncropped 1:100 drawings with technical material callout columns.
  - **16-Column:** High-density constructive plates accommodating multi-tiered dimension chains, structural grids, and bilingual annotations.
- **Materiality of Bone Paper & Graphite:** In digital light-mode presentation, blinding pure white (`#FFFFFF`) causes optical glare. Emulating archival paper (`#F8F8F5`) paired with deep graphite (`#111110`) and hairline drafting borders (`#DDD9D0`) conveys quiet tactile luxury.
