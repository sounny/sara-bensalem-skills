# 10 Deep Research Investigations: Sara Bensalem Skills Studio & Spatial Skill Architecture

> Studio Location: Sara Bensalem Studio • Strasbourg Atelier [48°35'05"N 07°45'02"E]  
> Production Site: [skills.sarabensalem.com](https://skills.sarabensalem.com)  
> Core Thesis: "Not a web design system. A suite of specialized physical design skills for coding agents."

---

## 1. Architectural Agentic Capabilities vs. Web UI Design Systems
**Research Objective:**  
Analyze the fundamental systemic divergence between generative web UI systems (Figma design tokens, CSS variables, React component libraries) and physical architectural intelligence (1:20 constructive assemblies, Eurocode/IBC structural load transfers, bioclimatic thermodynamic flows, and PMR egress geometries).  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- How can coding agents (Claude, Cursor, Antigravity) transcend 2D digital component generation to internalize physical material tectonics, tolerances, and constructive sequences?  
- What data schemas are required to represent constructive multi-layer wall envelopes (stone ashlar + lime-hemp biotamping + thermal break + timber glulam) in language models without loss of engineering rigor?

---

## 2. Anti-Render-Trap Ergonomics in High-Stakes Architectural Hiring
**Research Objective:**  
Investigate the cognitive vetting patterns of hiring partners and technical directors at tier-1 international architecture practices (Foster + Partners, Herzog & de Meuron, BIG, KPF, Renzo Piano Building Workshop, Snøhetta).  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- Why do photorealistic 3D render spreads (Lumion, Midjourney, V-Ray sunset atmospheres) trigger immediate skepticism and candidate rejection during 15-second portfolio triage?  
- How does the presence of an uncropped 1:20 constructive wall section detail (Plate IV) act as an undeniable signal of buildability, agency, and constructive competence?

## 3. Digital Swiss Grid Typographic Architecture for Monograph Publishing
**Research Objective:**  
Examine the editorial and layout mechanics of premier architectural monographs (*El Croquis*, *Detail Magazine*, *Lars Müller Publishers*, *2G Essays*).  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- How does the mathematical transition across 8-column, 12-column, and 16-column Swiss modular grids influence the hierarchy between technical drawings, dimension strings, and bilingual narrative captions?  
- What are the precise typographic ratios, baseline grid alignments (8pt vs 16pt), and whitespace discipline required to maintain publication luxury in interactive digital web environments?

## 4. Socratic Design Review Pedagogies Translated into AI Agents
**Research Objective:**  
Deconstruct the cross-examination methodologies utilized in final design reviews at premier architectural institutions (Harvard GSD, AA London, ETH Zürich, Bartlett UCL) and practice-level quality assurance juries.  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- How can an AI agent (`grill-my-design`) emulate the distinct intellectual interrogation styles of 4 jury archetypes: The Technical Partner (constructive failure modes & leaks), The 15s Recruiter (trust & provenance), The Crit Chair (spatial poetry & circulation), and The Environmental Auditor (thermodynamics & carbon)?  
- What scoring heuristics distinguish a superficial crit from an unsparing Socratic audit that pinpoints interstitial condensation, thermal bridges, and egress corridor pinch points?

---

## 5. Universal Accessibility (PMR) & Code Egress Geometric Verification
**Research Objective:**  
Synthesize international regulatory mandates for universal accessibility (French PMR / Arrêté du 24 décembre 2015, US ADA 2010 Standards, UK Approved Document M, IBC Chapter 10).  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- What algorithmic geometric checks can an agent execute on architectural floor plans (1:100) to verify 1500mm wheelchair turning radiuses, minimum 900mm door clearance passages, and unobstructed egress swing zones?  
- How can agentic tooling prevent code-compliance rejections before planning submission and permit reviews?

## 6. Bioclimatic Flow Simulation & Thermodynamic Representation
**Research Objective:**  
Formulate standards for communicating passive solar building physics and microclimate performance within 2D monograph plates.  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- How should vector diagrams represent solar altitude angles (summer solstice 65° vs winter solstice 18°), diurnal thermal mass radiative lag, and natural stack-effect buoyancy ventilation without visual clutter?  
- What physical metrics (U-value, g-value/SHGC, thermal lag phi, daylight factor DF) must accompany bioclimatic drawing plates to satisfy institutional environmental audits?

## 7. High-Craft Construction Detailing: 1:20 to 1:5 Tectonic Proof
**Research Objective:**  
Analyze constructive failure points in contemporary hybrid envelopes combining bio-based insulation (lime-hemp, wood fiber) with mass timber (glulam, CLT) and stone masonry.  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- Where are interstitial condensation dew points most likely to occur in breathing bio-composite exterior walls, and how are continuous thermal breaks and EPDM capillary flashing detailed at plinth and roof parapet junctions?  
- How do 1:5 interior millwork joinery drawings (shadow reveals, Blum/Hettich concealed hinges, acoustic baffles) reinforce end-to-end craft credibility?

## 9. Frictionless Agent Distribution: MCP, Cursor Rules & Local-First CLI
**Research Objective:**  
Evaluate architectural practice workflows for integrating AI coding assistants into daily design and production routines.  
**Key Research Questions:**  
- What are the comparative UX friction points between Model Context Protocol (MCP) JSON-RPC stdio servers (Claude Desktop, Cursor) vs native markdown skills (.gemini/skills/, .cursorrules, .windsurfrules)?  
- How can local-first distribution (zip bundles, offline execution, zero telemetry) assure architecture firms that their proprietary CAD/BIM geometries and project drawings remain completely confidential?
